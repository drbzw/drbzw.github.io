Prospective Students

I welcome applications from students interested in corporate finance, FinTech, capital markets, and behavioral finance for both Master's and PhD programs. I look for the following foundations and qualities:

First, a solid econometrics foundation — familiarity with multiple regression, panel data, and causal identification methods (difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity), together with strong data skills (Stata / Python / SAS);

Second, a systematic foundation in economic theory — coursework in intermediate microeconomics and macroeconomics, and the ability to frame research questions and interpret empirical findings through economic intuition;

Third, a solid foundation in financial theory — command of the core concepts and classic literature in corporate finance, investments, and asset pricing, and an understanding of the institutional background of Chinese capital markets;

Fourth, sustained enthusiasm and a rigorous attitude toward research, with the patience to go through the complete training cycle from topic selection, data, writing, to submission;

In addition, I expect students to be familiar with large language models and AI coding agents (e.g., Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex) in research, using cutting-edge tools to improve efficiency in literature reading, data processing, and writing.

Students under my supervision receive systematic training in research design, data analysis, paper writing, and conference presentations, and are supported to attend leading national and international conferences. Interested students are welcome to email a CV and transcripts to: wangzhao_cueb@163.com.
