Research & Publications

Research Interests

Corporate Finance
Corporate governance, information disclosure, investment efficiency, and capital structure — how governance shapes firms' information environments and financial decisions.
FinTech
Digital infrastructure, large language model embeddings in textual analysis, and the impact of digital transformation on disclosure and audit pricing.
Capital Markets
Market risk disclosure, stock price crash risk, analyst networks, and market microstructure.
Behavioral Finance
Disclosure strategies under political uncertainty, government officials' characteristics and corporate behavior, investor sentiment and risk perception.
