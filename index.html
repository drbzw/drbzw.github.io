王钊，首都经济贸易大学国际经济管理学院副教授，金融科技与管理科学系副主任。研究兴趣为公司金融、金融科技、资本市场与行为金融。 研究关注中国资本市场的信息环境与制度设计，涉及分析师网络、信息披露、公司治理、绿色金融与数字化转型等议题。

Zhao (Benny) Wang is an Associate Professor of Finance and Deputy Chair of the Department of FinTech and Management Science at the International School of Economics and Management, Capital University of Economics and Business. His research interests include corporate finance, FinTech, capital markets, and behavioral finance, with a focus on information environments and institutional design in Chinese capital markets, spanning analyst networks, information disclosure, corporate governance, green finance, and digital transformation.
